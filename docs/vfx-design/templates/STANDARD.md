# Standard contract template

Use for ordinary skills with a causal lifecycle, 4–8 layers, and at least one spatial or interaction dependency.

In addition to the Small requirements, the contract must declare transition continuity, layer-to-layer continuity, robustness seeds, and whether the effect is one-shot or sustained. Each moving source and each residual layer gets its own requirement/trace binding.

Use a Small template only when these declarations are genuinely inapplicable, not to avoid writing them.
