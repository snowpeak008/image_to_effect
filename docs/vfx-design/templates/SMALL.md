# Small contract template

Use for a single readable action with no more than three required visual layers. All ten contract segments remain mandatory.

- Lifecycle: four named stages and both completion/interrupt exits.
- Spatial/capture: inherit a frozen named capture profile; never omit seeds or camera identity.
- Layers: declare carrier, motion, timing and attachment for every required layer.
- Requirements: one authority per ID and at least one independent cross-evidence path.
- Budget: hard caps for particles, renderers, materials, lights, local texture residency and full dependency residency.
- Forbidden substitutions: list at least the relevant whole-image, static-trail, or fake-light prohibition.

Small does **not** mean “a static image with a name”.
